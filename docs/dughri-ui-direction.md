# Dughri Android UI — owner-approved direction

Mode: Operate. Targets: app/index.tsx, onboarding, permissions, settings, diagnostics, shared Screen components.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet daily-use utility, not a laboratory. One truthful status and next action lead; experimental controls live behind a separate diagnostics route.

OWN-WORLD: Owner selected “elegant and calm”: pure white and forest green, generous whitespace, native system typography with Arabic support, tonal Material buttons and restrained rounded surfaces. Dark colors follow the same roles. Owner subsequently supplied and approved the standalone mark for the UI/adaptive icon and square art for the legacy icon. Preserve this artwork.

STORY: Understand the purpose, inspect device access, and know why automatic mode is off. The call → shortcut → WhatsApp sequence explains intended behavior without implying verification.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark and settings link, a large two-line task headline, one forest-toned status area with the next action, then an unboxed three-step sequence. Permission details become clean divided rows on their own screen.

FORM: Initial seven grounded systems: address label, telephone switchboard, pocket guide, handset controls, checkpoints, service counter, Android quick-settings panel. Seed 168129a0 assigned the seventh. The owner rejected that proposed petrol-blue panel and explicitly pinned a quiet forest-green direction instead. Split-flap, dance score, Metro, reference-setting, zoo map, and ticket wallet challengers were declined for lower everyday Android familiarity and task clarity; retain their disciplines of readable states, sequence, typographic hierarchy, detail alignment, clear grouping, and explicit permissions progress, respectively. Code-led; no image-generation tool available.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
